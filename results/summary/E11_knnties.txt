CONCLUSION FOR THE MANUSCRIPT
  The KNN column is not bit-reproducible across machines at fixed library versions, because the
  published hyperparameters do not determine the neighbourhood when distances tie, and ties are
  pervasive on binary fingerprints under a Euclidean metric. The spread above is the honest
  uncertainty on every KNN figure; it is of the same order as the primary contrast itself, so no
  KNN-specific direction may be claimed. SVM and GBM reproduced bit-identically and RF to within
  5.4e-4 RMSE, so this is a KNN-specific limitation, not a general failure of the pipeline.
  It is also an instance of the paper's own thesis: a benchmark contract that omits an
  implementation detail leaves the reported number under-determined.
